**Figure 4.** Accuracy of retained answers as the highest-entropy answers are rejected, by model family (rows) and difficulty tier (columns). A rising curve means semantic entropy ranks wrong answers above right ones. The vertical line marks the pre-registered 0.20 rejection fraction. Curves stop at 80% rejection, beyond which fewer than twenty items remain; the summary statistics use the full curve. Accuracy is the strict grading (n = 100 per configuration per tier); the lenient grading is in Table A3. Colour and line style both identify the compression level here, whereas in Figures 1–3 colour identifies the model family.
